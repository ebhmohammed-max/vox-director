The six current rendered anchors are in `anchors/`. These repo-composed prompts document the repeatable style lane for any future regeneration; they were not used as a tool transcript for the current images.

## A01 — LIGHT THROUGH WATER

A premium Vox-style editorial documentary keyframe, a tactile paper-collage board photographed straight-on with physically layered depth. The dominant hero is a cinematic, physically based 3D documentary image printed as a photographic cut-out with realistic volumetric lighting, fine material detail, and a soft drop shadow. SCENE: A vast empty underwater water column viewed at cinematic wide scale, layered blue-green depth, soft suspended particulate, several distinct volumetric sun shafts descending from the surface and fading into deep navy water. No seabed, no structures, no objects, no silhouettes.. FRAMING: wide establishing view, strong depth planes, brightest shafts off-center, generous quiet dark negative space. LIGHTING: cool top light refracted through water, subtle volumetric rays, soft falloff into navy shadow. PALETTE: deep teal and midnight-navy shadows, cool blue-green ambient fill, subtle bronze and amber highlights only where motivated. Surround and overlap the hero image with a few restrained physical editorial layers: torn cream paper, dark navy printed-paper fields, cropped abstract non-locatable map or document fragments, one small blank annotation card, discreet translucent tape, decorative brass pushpins, and a thin red thread. Visible paper fibers, crisp torn edges, believable paper relief and careful shadows. The 3D image remains the clear focal subject; keep the layout sophisticated and uncluttered with negative space for separately typeset captions. No legible text, numbers, coordinates, logos, watermark, UI, or fabricated source labels. Do not flatten the composition into a slide and do not turn the whole frame into a plain CGI environment. Evidence boundary: anonymous atmospheric water only; do not show an archaeological site, seabed, ruin, palace, artifact, ring, inscription, people, equipment, or a recognizable geographic location; this is not field evidence. Aspect ratio 16:9.
